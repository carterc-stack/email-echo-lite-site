Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Email Echo Lite ("Echo", "we") is a shared support inbox with optional AI-drafted replies. This policy explains what we collect when you use Echo, why, and what control you have over it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them to create your account and workspace.
  • Gmail data. When you connect a mailbox, Echo uses the Gmail API (gmail.modify) to read messages and keep the inbox in sync. We store the cleaned text of messages, their sender, recipients, subject and dates. We do not store attachments or raw message files.
    • Threads from the last 30 days become your team's inbox.
    • Past conversations that contain a reply from your business are used to build a private reference library for your workspace, so drafts match how your team writes.
  • Gmail access tokens. The token that lets Echo access your mailbox is stored encrypted, in a secrets vault, and is never shown to anyone.
  • Usage information. Basic records such as last sign-in time and draft counts, used to run the service and enforce plan limits.

How we use it

  • To show, search and sync your team's inbox.
  • To generate reply drafts for your workspace when you ask for them. You review every draft, and nothing is sent without action from your team.
  • To send you service emails, such as "import complete" or a warning before inactive data is deleted.

Each workspace's data is used only for that workspace. Your emails are never used to draft replies for any other business.

Google user data: Limited Use

Email Echo Lite's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Gmail data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of Echo described above.
  • We do not sell Gmail data, and we do not use or transfer it for advertising.
  • We do not use Gmail data to train general-purpose AI models.
  • No person reads your Gmail data unless you give us permission (for example, to help with a support request), it is needed for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Echo. Each one processes data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and background jobs, in the United States. Search indexing of your messages also runs inside Supabase.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that writes drafts. When a draft is requested, the relevant conversation and reference examples are sent to Anthropic's API to write it. Anthropic does not train its models on this data.
  • Google provides sign-in and the Gmail API.

Retention and deletion

  • Inbox threads cover a rolling 30-day window.
  • If nobody in a workspace signs in for 180 days, we delete that workspace's threads, reference library, search indexes and stored Gmail tokens. We send warning emails before this happens.
  • You can ask us to delete your workspace and all its data at any time by emailing the address below. We will confirm when it is done.
  • You can revoke Echo's access to your Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Echo then stops syncing that mailbox right away.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database access rules keep each workspace's data separate, and access tokens are kept in an encrypted vault.

Children

Echo is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For significant changes we will also notify workspace owners by email.
