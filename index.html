Privacy

A free shared inbox for small support teams.

Connect your support Gmail, and your team works the inbox together. When you want it, Echo drafts replies that sound like you, learned from the replies your team has already sent.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and create a workspace for your business.
  2. Connect your support mailbox. Echo imports recent conversations so your inbox is ready right away.
  3. Answer as a team. The inbox is free. AI drafts are optional, and you always review a draft before anything is sent.

Your data

Echo only uses your mail to run your own inbox and drafts. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with other workspaces. Read the full privacy policy.
